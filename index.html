    Print colour (photos only)

    Vintage paper only textures the paper. Your photos stay sharp unless you add film grain. For the cleanest photos, use Photo tone “Natural colour” and film grain 0.

    03

    Lay out the pages

    Photos

    Drop photos here

    On a phone: tap any empty photo spot on a page to add a photo from your gallery or camera. Or tap a photo here, then tap a page. On a computer you can also drag photos onto pages.

    Your photos are autosaved with the draft on this device, so a reload keeps them. Press Save to keep the project in your projects.

    Page order

    Drag a page onto another to swap them, or tap one page and then another.

    04

    Print it

    Higher dpi only helps if your photo quality is 4K or Original. 600 dpi files are large and may not export on a phone.

      Home printers leave a white edge of about 4 mm, so edge-to-edge photos look best from a print shop. Pages with margins print cleanly at home.
